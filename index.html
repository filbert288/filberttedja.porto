Research

Journal-style paper · Team of 5 · Information Systems, BINUS

Analysis of the Effectiveness of Flood Detection Features in the JAKI Application as a Flood Mitigation Effort

JakPantau is the real-time flood map inside the JAKI public service app. We surveyed users and tested the DeLone and McLean Information System Success Model with SEM-PLS in SmartPLS 4, to see which quality dimension drives satisfaction most.

190+valid respondents
0.465service quality effect, the strongest
3 of 3hypotheses accepted

Information quality (0.362) and system quality (0.287) also had significant positive effects. The measurement model held up: indicator loadings from 0.854 to 0.960 and Cronbach's alpha above 0.96. We recommended responsive user support, accurate flood data, and faster, easier-to-use screens.

My role: I designed the survey questionnaire, helped analyse the results, wrote the abstract, drafted parts of the introduction, literature review and methodology, and edited the full manuscript.

  • SEM-PLS
  • SmartPLS 4
  • Survey design
  • E-government

Database practicum · Team of 5 · Even semester 2024/2025

LocalFarmer: a sales and purchase database in SQL

Designed the schema for a farm-produce business with eight tables (farmers, customers, staff, products, product types, and purchase and sales transactions with their detail tables). Primary and foreign keys keep relations valid, and regex and check constraints enforce the rules: ID formats like FR001 and CU001, email domains per role, phone numbers starting with 08, transaction dates limited to 2024, and quantity ranges.

Populated every table with sample data using INSERT ALL, then wrote reporting queries with multi-table joins, GROUP BY with aggregates, HAVING filters, subqueries against the average quantity, and string and date formatting (SUBSTR, REPLACE, UPPER, TO_CHAR).

  • SQL (DDL and DML)
  • Constraints and regex
  • Joins
  • Aggregation
  • Subqueries
